# V6 visual update

This release replaces generic/placeholder visuals with project-specific research imagery supplied by Pouria Maleki.

## Added research visuals

- Iranian Vehicle Images Dataset: dataset statistics + Hamedan traffic detection example
- Object Detection for Vehicles with YOLO: road, ambulance, fire-truck and evaluation-result figures
- Flood Risk Analysis: research overview figure
- Sustainable Energy Management: fuzzy membership, power-demand and temperature figures
- Intelligent Traffic Control: animated intersection/discretization figure
- Embedded / audio-processing prototype photo
- Electronics teaching and laboratory photos
- B.Sc. HVAC-control Simulink figure

## UI changes

- Blog posts can now include a responsive research-figure gallery with bilingual captions and descriptive alt text.
- Research cards on the home page use real project imagery for all featured research areas.
- The About page now includes a visual “Work in practice” section for teaching, laboratory work and prototyping.
- Traffic-control case study can show more than two research figures, including the animated simulation.

## Image handling

Most supplied PNG/JPEG assets were converted to optimized WebP files for faster loading while preserving the original GIF animation for the traffic environment.
