Hello! I'm Pouria Maleki (پوریا ملکی), an Electrical Engineer specializing in Control Systems from Hamedan, Iran. My work sits at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Deep Reinforcement Learning, and real-world control engineering challenges.

With a Master's degree from Bu Ali Sina University (GPA 3.91/4, Rank 1), I've channeled my academic foundation into both research and hands-on teaching. I currently teach electronics and programming at the Ministry of Education while actively contributing to IEEE-published research.

My mission is to advance intelligent systems that are not just technically rigorous but genuinely impactful — from smarter urban traffic control to sustainable energy management.
